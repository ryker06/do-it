copied: the soft pastel cover band at the top of each card with a quiet glyph centered, the calm paper body below it with a single dominant figure, the inline pill row at the foot for direct manipulation.

adapted: Element 1's ± buttons become a number input + black "log" pill (we record values, not increment counts). Layout 5's metric phrase becomes "current → target · weeks left". layout notes

card anatomy: pastel cover band (80px) with quiet glyph + optional rotated chip → identity line (SF Pro Display 21/700) → metric phrase ("96kg → 100kg · 2w left", tabular nums) → hairline track with single ink dot AT current position → 12-week mini-grid receipts below → echo line ("logged 96kg. → 100kg coming.") → inline log row at foot.

progress is NOT a fill bar. It's a hairline + a single 10px ink dot. The 12-week mini-grid below adds the "I see weeks of evidence" feel. This is the entire signature of the screen — one position dot, not a percent.

vision grouping: small uppercase header ("vision · 220kg deadlifter") with hairline rule. multiple goals can roll up to one vision.

tone strings: "I am a 100kg deadlifter." (identity) / "96kg → 100kg · 2w left" (metric) / "logged 96kg. → 100kg coming." (save echo) / "+ goal" (CTA). lowercase. period at end. fact → future-self.

memoji (LOCKED): jay.webp?color=7 only. topbar: rotating motivational quote — "today is the lift." / "ready when you are." — never a personal greeting.
